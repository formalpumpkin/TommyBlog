I just found my first true Wrinkle.

The kind that doesn't go away when my face relaxes.

I didn't think I'd get to see,

Wrinkles on my face

I didn't think I'd make it this far.

And I always wondered,

When I do get Wrinkles,

Because every wrinkle

And over and over again

And mine?

Mine is from when I smile. It's from when I laugh.

And it's right where the sun hit. And I feel really blessed.

And I feel really happy.

It's my wrinkle.

It's my first one.
