According to Aaron, there were a couple rules we had to follow while tubing. Park rangers would be patrolling to make sure we had whistles, life vests, and inner tubes with handles. We didn’t run into any, but I'm still glad we came prepared. We were somehow able to come up with almost everything apart from rope and a few inner tubes which we bought at Walmart. Thank God Sam had life vests. It’s a 2.25 hour drive from Greenbelt and the perfect amount of secluded. The inner tubes with handles made it easy to tie our rafts together. The life jackets were good for a nice swim so Kai, Sam and me could tread to the shore and look for driftwood. The park was a northern part of the Potomac river, close enough to Frostburg that Lauryn could drive down and join us. Since Laryn brought her car so we could avoid walking up the river. Now that I’ve made it to Italy again, I’m having a great time. The workaway is on a campsite only 5 kilometers from the beach and a 10 minute walk from some great outdoor rock climbing. I definitely feel less secluded than last summer, it's easy to access the city since someone is always willing to drive us. So far I've been to the beach and visited Torino to meet Owen. The people I'm working with are also amazing people.

Street beer in Torino. Having pizza on the beach with everyone

The work is hard but not grueling. We start work at 8:30 and work until 1pm when we eat lunch. Someone– typically Camilla, our organizer– prepares lunch. It's always delish and includes vegetarian options. The Artichoke season just ended and the zucchini season is very bountiful. We’ve been partaking in lots of fried zucchini flowers. Usually work consists of harvesting, pruning, watering, hedging, tilling, and collecting weeds from the hedging to feed to the goats. The easy work so far has been taking stems off the mulberries for jam, and taking seeds out of loquats. The loquats go to jam and we use the seeds to make an alcohol called nespolino which you drink after a meal for digestion. It's strong as hell. We also have to peel the loquat seeds… five kilos.

A look at the garden. Us peeling 5 kilos of fawking seeds

One of my favorite parts of this workaway has been that we speak a lot of Italian. Camilla(Camie)’s family only speaks Italian and they really like speaking with us. I think so far I’ve spoken more Italian my first week than in my lifetime. I’m excited for the rest of my time in Europe, I’m contemplating visiting Nice for a weekend since it's so close.
